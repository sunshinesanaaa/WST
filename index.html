Our system

How Style Sync works

Style Sync is the web-based appointment and scheduling system developed for Style Haven Salon. It replaces phone-tag booking with a single, organised calendar.

1

Choose a service

Browse the menu and pick the treatment you need — prices and durations are shown upfront.

2

Pick date & stylist

Style Sync displays only the time slots that are still open for your chosen specialist.

3

Get confirmed

The system blocks double bookings automatically and issues an appointment reference.

4

Track & manage

Front-desk staff can confirm, complete or cancel bookings and view daily reports.

Built for the front desk

One calendar instead of four notebooks

Before Style Sync, bookings arrived through phone calls, text messages and social-media DMs — each written into a different notebook. Staff had to cross-check by hand, which led to double bookings and forgotten appointments.

  • Automatic detection of conflicting schedules
  • Daily and weekly appointment views
  • Status tracking: pending, confirmed, completed, cancelled
  • Simple reports on busy days and top services
