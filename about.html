Company background

From three chairs to seven stations

Style Haven Salon opened its doors in 2019 along Aurora Boulevard in Cubao, Quezon City. Founders Ana Villanueva and Clara Domingo — a hair stylist and a nail technician — started with only three chairs, one sink and a simple idea: that a salon visit should feel calm, honest and punctual.

As word spread through the neighbourhood, the salon added skin and makeup services and grew into a seven-station studio staffed by five specialists. Today Style Haven serves more than twelve thousand clients a year, from students dropping in for a quick trim to brides preparing for their big day.

Growth, however, brought a problem. Bookings arrived by phone call, text message and social-media direct message, and each was written into a different notebook. Staff had to cross-check them by hand, which caused double bookings, forgotten appointments and long waits at the counter. To solve this, the salon commissioned Style Sync — a web-based appointment and scheduling system that keeps every booking in one organised, conflict-free calendar.

Our mission

To make professional beauty care accessible, affordable and stress-free for every client by combining skilled hands with smart, simple technology.

Our vision

To become the most trusted neighbourhood salon in Quezon City — known for consistent quality, transparent pricing and appointments that always start on time.

Our values

Cleanliness above all, honesty about what suits you, respect for every client's budget, and continuous improvement through technology.
